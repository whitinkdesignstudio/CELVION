Frequently Asked Questions

What International Quality & Compliance Certifications Do CELVION Materials Hold?

CELVION products are certified under ISO 9001:2015 Quality Management Systems, ISO 14001 Environmental Standards, FSC® Chain of Custody, BPI Compostable, and OK Compost Home/Industrial. All products comply fully with US FDA 21 CFR 176.170, EU Regulation 10/2011, and FSSAI food contact safety norms.

Can Enterprise Buyers Request Batch-Specific Certificates of Analysis (CoA)?

Yes. Our Quality Assurance laboratory conducts rigorous lot-level quality checks for every manufacturing batch. Enterprise buyers can request a lot-specific Certificate of Analysis (CoA) detailing basis weight, Cobb water absorption rate, kit oil resistance rating, and heavy metal testing with their shipment.

Are CELVION Materials 100% PFAS-Free and Fluorinated Chemical-Free?

Absolutely. All CELVION packaging solutions feature 100% plant-derived bio-barrier coatings engineered with zero intentionally added PFAS, PFOA, PFOS, or fluorinated chemicals. Our materials pass NABL third-party total fluorine laboratory testing (< 50 ppm) complying with EU Single-Use Plastics directives and US state laws.

How Can Corporate Procurement Teams Download Technical Data Sheets (TDS) & Compliance Dossiers?

Technical Data Sheets (TDS), Safety Data Sheets (SDS), and Food Contact Declarations of Compliance (DoC) can be downloaded directly from the Resource Center on this page or requested directly from your dedicated CELVION technical account engineer.

What In-House Quality Assurance & Lab Testing Protocols Are Conducted?

Our in-house NABL-aligned laboratory conducts continuous inline automated optical inspection, edge wick testing, burst strength and tensile analysis, TAPPI T 559 grease resistance kit testing, and overall migration testing (EN 1186 / EN 13130) prior to dispatch.
