Precision testing & uncompromising standards

Every product carrying the CELVION mark represents our global reputation. Our quality management system combines continuous optical inspection, calibrated density analysis, and strict international compliance.

Pillar 01

Engineering Excellence

Every plate, bowl, and tray is engineered with precise wall thickness and reinforced rim geometry for high structural integrity under heavy thermal loads.

Pillar 02

Toxin-Free & Pure Fibre

Formulated with 100% renewable sugarcane and bamboo fibres with zero PFAS, heavy metals, or plastic coatings, fulfilling global food safety directives.

Pillar 03

Automated Inline Inspection

Real-time optical vision scanners check 100% of manufactured units for surface uniformity, edge trim accuracy, and dimensional tolerances.

Pillar 04

Thermal & Liquid Resistance

Batch-tested for hot oil (120°C) and boiling water (100°C) retention up to 4 hours without leakage, softening, or structural deformation.

Pillar 05

Traceable Batch Coding

Every export shipping carton features unique QR codes linking directly to raw material origin, manufacturing date, and lab quality test reports.

Pillar 06

Export-Grade Protection

Heavy-duty double-wall master cartons and moisture-barrier shrink wraps designed to withstand sea freight vibration and humidity extremes.
