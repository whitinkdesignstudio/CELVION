Customised Packaging Development & Manufacturing Process

From an initial requirement to production-ready packaging, CELVION works through a structured development process to align product function, material selection, design and manufacturing requirements.

01

Requirement & Application

Understand the product, application, dimensions, performance needs, packaging format and target market.

02

Product / Structure Development

Develop the packaging structure and key specifications around the intended use, handling and presentation requirements.

03

Material Selection

Select suitable renewable, fibre-based or other applicable materials based on product requirements and production feasibility.

04

Prototype & Sampling

Create samples or prototypes for fit, function, appearance and practical evaluation.

05

Testing & Approval

Review the agreed specifications, sample performance and required quality parameters before production approval.

06

Production

Move the approved design into controlled production using defined specifications and manufacturing parameters.

07

Quality Control

Inspect finished packaging for agreed dimensions, appearance, workmanship and consistency.

08

Packing & Dispatch

Pack finished products according to customer requirements and prepare them for shipment to the required destination.

How We Will Handle Your Product

Every shipment moves through four controlled packaging levels, engineered for product protection, logistics efficiency, and consistent brand presentation from our facility to your port of destination.

Product

01
Every product is manufactured to defined specifications, engineered for strength, consistency, and dimensional accuracy.

Primary Packaging

02
Individual products are packed to help prevent damage during handling, stacking, and transit.

Secondary Packaging

03
Products are grouped into cartons with piece count, weight, dimensions, and labelling aligned to the order specifications.

Transport Packaging

04
Packed cartons are prepared for efficient container loading, with shipping labels and required documentation organised for dispatch and export.
