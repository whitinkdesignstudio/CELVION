Frequently Asked Questions

Which Countries and Continents Does CELVION Export To?

CELVION exports containerized shipments to over 50 countries across North America, Europe, the Middle East, Asia-Pacific, and Africa, including USA, UK, UAE, Australia, and Mauritius.

What Incoterms and Shipping Terms Does CELVION Offer for International Orders?

We support standard international trade terms including FOB (Free on Board), CIF (Cost, Insurance & Freight), and CFR (Cost and Freight) based on buyer preferences and port destinations.

What is the Standard Lead Time for Export Shipments?

Standard catalog items ship within 2 to 3 weeks upon order confirmation and payment. Custom OEM mould production and private label branding orders typically ship in 4 to 6 weeks.

What Export Documentation and Compliance Certificates Are Provided?

Each export order includes Bill of Lading, Commercial Invoice, Packing List, Certificate of Origin, Phytosanitary Certificate, and Batch Certificate of Analysis (CoA) for customs clearance.

What Are the Minimum Order Quantities (MOQ) for Container Shipping?

Our standard export MOQ is 1 Full Container Load (20ft or 40ft High Cube container), which can be customized with mixed SKU product assortments across all CELVION product families.
