CELVION global innovation platforms

Our innovation strategy is structured around four distinct platforms designed to advance
material performance, customer co-engineering, and industry thought leadership.

Platform 01

CELVION Lab™

Applied research and development focusing on next-generation cellulose composites, natural binder matrixes, and food-grade moisture barrier science.

Platform 02

CELVION Innovation Centre™

Direct customer collaboration hub for rapid 3D CAD prototyping, custom mould creation, and bespoke structural package testing.

Platform 03

CELVION Academy™

Educational framework providing technical knowledge-sharing, material science training, and packaging sustainability workshops for enterprise clients.

Platform 04

CELVION Sustainability Council™

Internal leadership governing corporate ESG policies, raw material auditing, circular economy strategies, and carbon reduction benchmarks.

Measurable environmental impact without clichés

At CELVION, credibility comes from specificity. We replace vague slogans with precise, verifiable material science metrics and circular end-of-life pathways.

100% Upcycled Agricultural Fibre

Derived entirely from renewable sugarcane bagasse and rapidly renewable bamboo stalk residues, diverting agricultural waste from open-air incineration.

Zero Microplastic Contamination

Formulated without synthetic PE/PLA laminations. Naturally breaks down in commercial compost facilities into organic nutrient soil within 90 days.

Closed-Loop Process Water

Our manufacturing facilities feature 100% recycled process water circuits, minimizing freshwater consumption and ensuring zero toxic liquid discharge.
