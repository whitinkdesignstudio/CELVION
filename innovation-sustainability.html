Empirical Sustainability Without Cliches

At CELVION, credibility comes from empirical specificity. We replace vague slogans with precise, verifiable material science metrics and circular end-of-life pathways.

100% Upcycled Agricultural Fibre

Derived entirely from renewable sugarcane bagasse and rapidly renewable agricultural stalk residues, diverting agricultural waste from open-air burning.

Zero Microplastic Contamination

Formulated without synthetic PE or PLA laminations. Naturally breaks down in commercial compost facilities into organic nutrient soil within 90 days.

Closed-Loop Water & Zero Discharge

Our manufacturing facilities feature 100% recycled process water circuits, minimizing freshwater consumption and ensuring zero toxic liquid discharge.

Frequently Asked Questions

How Fast Do CELVION Plant-Fibre Products Compost and Decompose?

CELVION products naturally break down into organic nutrient-rich soil within 60 to 90 days in commercial or home composting conditions, leaving zero microplastic residues or toxic chemicals.

Are CELVION Products 100% PFAS-Free and Fluorine-Free?

Yes, our material science team formulates fluorine-free barrier technology to deliver superior water and oil resistance without using harmful PFAS or synthetic wax coatings.

How Does Plant-Fibre Packaging Reduce Corporate Carbon Footprint?

Plant fibres sequester carbon dioxide during growth and require significantly less processing energy, reducing lifecycle greenhouse gas emissions by up to 80% compared to traditional petroleum plastics.

Are CELVION Packaging Products Microwaveable and Freezer-Safe?

Yes, our engineered plant-fibre matrix withstands extreme temperatures from -20°C up to +120°C, making products ideal for hot food service, reheating, and deep-freeze storage.

What is CELVION's Circular Economy Strategy?

We upcycle renewable agricultural byproducts, engineer zero-waste production systems, and design fully compostable end-of-life products that safely return organic nutrients to the earth.
