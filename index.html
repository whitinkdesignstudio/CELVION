Frequently Asked Questions

What products does CELVION offer?

CELVION focuses on two main categories: bagasse tableware such as plates, bowls, trays and food containers, and sustainable molded-fiber packaging including protective trays, inserts and customized packaging components for different products and industries.

Why should businesses consider sustainable tableware and packaging?

Sustainable tableware and packaging can help businesses reduce their dependence on conventional plastic and other less sustainable materials while responding to growing customer, retailer and regulatory expectations. The right fiber-based solution can also provide practical performance without compromising usability.

What can CELVION offer businesses looking to switch to sustainable tableware or packaging?

CELVION focuses on bagasse tableware and customized molded-fiber packaging solutions for businesses seeking practical alternatives to conventional materials. Depending on the application, we can support standard products as well as customized packaging developed around specific product, protection and presentation requirements.

Are CELVION sugarcane bagasse products microwave- and freezer-safe?

Yes, CELVION sugarcane bagasse tableware is engineered to withstand extreme temperatures ranging from -20°C in freezers up to 120°C in microwaves and ovens. Made from high-density sugarcane pulp fibers, our plates, bowls, and compartment trays maintain structural integrity without warping, leaking, or softening.

What is the typical lead time and MOQ for custom OEM packaging & export orders?

CELVION provides flexible Minimum Order Quantities (MOQ) and rapid production cycles for global B2B buyers. Standard export container orders dispatch within 3 to 5 business days for in-stock items. For custom-molded packaging and OEM private-label orders, prototype tooling requires 10 to 15 days, followed by bulk container production.
