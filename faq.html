Bagasse Tableware & Products

What is bagasse, and how is it used in CELVION products?

+

Bagasse is the natural fibrous residue remaining after sugarcane juice extraction. CELVION processes this 100% renewable plant fiber into high-performance molded tableware and protective packaging. It serves as a durable, eco-friendly replacement for single-use plastics and polystyrene foam.

Why choose bagasse tableware instead of conventional disposable tableware?

+

Bagasse tableware is manufactured from renewable sugarcane fiber, offering superior structural strength compared to paper or plastic. It provides high heat, oil, and moisture resistance while supporting corporate ESG goals by reducing single-use plastic waste.

What products does CELVION offer?

+

CELVION specializes in two primary product categories: molded bagasse tableware including plates, compartment trays, bowls, and clamshell boxes; and custom-engineered molded-fiber protective packaging designed for industrial, retail, and commercial applications.

Are CELVION sugarcane bagasse products microwave- and freezer-safe?

+

Yes, CELVION sugarcane bagasse tableware is engineered to withstand extreme temperatures ranging from -20°C in freezers up to 120°C in microwaves and ovens. Made from high-density sugarcane pulp fibers, our plates, bowls, and compartment trays maintain structural integrity without warping, leaking, or softening.

Safety, Compliance & Sustainability

Why should businesses consider sustainable tableware and packaging?

+

Switching to fiber-based sustainable packaging helps companies ensure regulatory compliance as single-use plastic bans expand globally. It lowers carbon footprint, satisfies growing consumer preference for eco-friendly brands, and maintains uncompromised functional performance.

Are CELVION products food-contact safe?

+

Yes, all CELVION tableware products are manufactured under strict hygienic standards using food-grade natural sugarcane fiber. They comply with international food-contact safety standards (including FDA and US/EU regulations) and are completely free from harmful PFAS and toxic chemicals.

Are CELVION bagasse products compostable?

+

Yes, CELVION bagasse products are 100% biodegradable and commercially compostable. Under proper composting conditions, they naturally break down into nutrient-rich organic soil within 60 to 90 days without leaving toxic microplastics or chemical residues.

Why should businesses switch to sustainable molded-fiber packaging?

+

Molded-fiber packaging provides custom-contoured protection for delicate goods, replacing plastic blister packs and EPS foam. It delivers high shock absorption, reduces packaging volume, and demonstrates corporate commitment to circular economy practices.

Customized Packaging & OEM Solutions

What types of products can CELVION sustainable packaging be designed for?

+

CELVION custom packaging can be developed for cosmetics, personal care items, electronics, FMCG products, glass bottles, toys, and retail goods requiring product-specific protection or presentation.

Can CELVION packaging replace plastic packaging?

+

In many suitable applications, molded-fiber packaging effectively replaces plastic trays, inserts, blister packs, and EPS foam. The optimal solution is engineered based on product geometry, shock absorption requirements, and technical feasibility.

Can CELVION develop customized packaging?

+

Yes. CELVION offers end-to-end custom development based on product dimensions, weight, protection requirements, and application goals. Custom trays, inserts, and protective molded-fiber packaging formats are engineered to order.

Can CELVION provide OEM or private-label solutions?

+

Yes, subject to minimum order specifications. CELVION supports B2B clients, distributors, and retail brands with customized product molding, branded packaging formats, and private-label manufacturing.

Ordering, Export & Samples

What can CELVION offer businesses looking to switch to sustainable tableware or packaging?

+

CELVION offers full technical support, certified food-safe products, custom packaging design, and competitive volume pricing. We assist partners through every stage of material transition, from initial sample testing to full-scale container dispatch.

Can CELVION supply products for international markets?

+

Yes. CELVION serves domestic and international B2B markets. Standard export orders start at one 20-ft or 40-ft container with flexible product mix options, shipped via major Indian ports such as Mundra, Nhava Sheva (JNPT), and Chennai.
