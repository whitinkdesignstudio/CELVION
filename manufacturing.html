The Engineering Process

From sugarcane bagasse to finished product, every stage is engineered for
consistency, strength, and reliability, one deliberate step at a time.

01

Bagasse Sourcing

Sugarcane bagasse, an agricultural by-product, collected as our primary renewable fibre.

02

Fibre Preparation

Cleaned and refined to achieve consistent fibre quality.

03

Pulp Formation & Moulding

Fibres converted into pulp and precision-moulded into shape.

04

Pressing & Drying

Controlled heat and pressure for consistent strength and finish.

05

Quality Inspection

Rigorously tested for dimensions, durability, and structural integrity.

06

Packing & Global Dispatch

Export-grade packed and dispatched worldwide, on time.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can CELVION develop customized packaging?

Yes. CELVION can work on customized packaging concepts based on the product's dimensions, shape, weight, protection requirements and intended application. Custom trays, inserts, protective components and molded-fiber packaging formats can be developed as required.

Are CELVION bagasse products suitable for hot, cold, oily and liquid foods?

Bagasse tableware is designed for a wide range of hot and cold food applications, including foods containing moisture, oil and grease. Actual temperature and application limits depend on the specific product specification.
