Frequently Asked Questions

Why should businesses switch to sustainable molded-fiber packaging?

Molded-fiber packaging can replace suitable plastic, foam and other conventional packaging components where protection, fit and functionality can be achieved with fiber. It can be customized around the product, helping businesses reduce conventional plastic use while maintaining practical packaging performance.

What types of products can CELVION sustainable packaging be designed for?

Packaging can be developed for products such as cosmetics, personal care items, electronics, FMCG products, bottles, glass products, toys, retail products and other items requiring product-specific protection or presentation.

Can CELVION packaging replace plastic packaging?

In many suitable applications, molded-fiber packaging can replace plastic trays, inserts, blister-style components, foam/EPS and other conventional protective packaging. The final solution depends on the product's shape, protection requirements, handling conditions and technical feasibility.
